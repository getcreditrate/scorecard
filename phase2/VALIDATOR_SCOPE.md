# Independent Validator Scope of Work, version 1.0

Prepared 20 September 2026 under Technical Architecture s.7.2 and Model Risk Management Policy s.6, which bar live use until independent external validation has been commissioned.

1. **Independence.** No role in developing the framework; no financial interest in the Foundation or any partner institution; reports to the board's second line review function, not to the model developer.
2. **Documents under review.** Technical Architecture and Four Phase Implementation Plan; Model Validation Report with Addendum; Model Card; Regulatory Compliance Mapping Register; Model Risk Management Policy; Retrospective Validation Protocol; Phase 2 Validation Report when produced; this repository.
3. **Scope.** (a) Conceptual soundness of the architecture for the stated use. (b) Adherence of the Phase 2 Validation Report to the frozen protocol and materiality of any deviation. (c) Replication of headline discrimination and calibration results from code and records. (d) Adequacy of fair lending tests and any less discriminatory alternative analysis. (e) Completeness of stated limitations. (f) Whether reason codes satisfy Regulation B adverse action requirements.
4. **Deliverables.** Written validation report with findings graded by severity; findings register; Foundation's written management response to each finding before any recommendation for pilot use.
5. **Candidate categories.** Academic researchers in credit risk or fair lending; model risk consultancies practising under SR 11-7; former bank or consumer financial protection examiners in private practice.
6. **Timing.** Engaged at the start of Phase 2B; report delivered before the Phase 2 exit criterion is assessed.
